# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the
work.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported through the private security/contact channel listed in
`SECURITY.md`. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
that do not align with this standard.
